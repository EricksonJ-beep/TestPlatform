*For Jon's review, Nov 5 2026. Same rules as the Physical Science list: once approved, the first choice becomes each target's core video and the backup its extension. Test each pick on a student account (Restricted Mode) first.*

**At a glance**
- Every target has a first choice and a backup. Most picks are Amoeba Sisters and Crash Course Biology. TED-Ed, Bozeman, FuseSchool and Khan Academy fill the gaps.
- **Worth recording your own:** U1.LT3 (your microscopes and focusing order), U4.LT4 (your Elodea or leaf-disk setup), U5.LT3 (building a karyotype), and probably U8.LT3 (your plant-like / animal-like / fungus-like protists).
- **Check against what you teach:**
  - U1.LT1: Crash Course calls a hypothesis a "tentative explanation", not your If…then prediction.
  - U4.LT1: newer videos say about 30–32 ATP per glucose, and you teach 36.
  - U4.LT2: many videos treat anaerobic respiration and fermentation as different things.
  - U6.LT2: needs about three clips for the five patterns, and the blood-type clip may write the alleles as Iᴬ/Iᴮ/i.
- **Brand-new Amoeba Sisters uploads (2026):** Mitosis, Meiosis and Karyotypes. Test these in Restricted Mode first.
- **Shared video:** U8.LT3 and U8.LT4 use the two halves of one Amoeba Sisters "Protists and Fungi" video. Each would get its own start time.

Found with WebSearch limited to youtube.com, 2026-11-05. Every URL below showed up in a search result. No IDs were guessed. Each video was checked against the `read` option in that target's relearning pack (`content/packs/bio-uX-ltY-relearning/pack.json`). Notes on the method:

- **Channel**: "confirmed" means the result's title or description named the channel (for example "...Crash Course Biology #5", "Explore ... with the Amoeba Sisters!", "Introduction to pH | Biology | Khan Academy"). "Per search" means the search grouped or credited the video to that channel but the snippet shown didn't name it. "Channel unconfirmed" means nothing named the uploader. For Bozeman Science, several descriptions name **Paul Andersen** but not the channel. Those are marked "Bozeman Science (description names Paul Andersen)".
- **Length**: search results almost never showed run time. The "~min" figures are from general knowledge of the series: Amoeba Sisters usually 5–9 min, FuseSchool 3–5, Bozeman 7–12, original Crash Course Biology (2012) 10–15. "Length unknown" means no basis at all. Check each video against the ~4 min core + ~6 min extension.
- **Restricted Mode**: can't be tested from here. Amoeba Sisters, Crash Course, FuseSchool, Khan Academy and TED-Ed usually pass. Test each "per search" or unconfirmed video on a student account.
- **URLs with extra parameters**: some IDs appeared only with extra URL parameters (`&vl=en`, `&pp=...`, `?amp=&...`). They're noted in the table, and the URL is given with the parameters removed. Check that link.
