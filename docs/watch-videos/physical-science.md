# Physical Science "Watch" relearning candidates (all 25 targets)

*Approved by Jon Nov 5 2026: First choice is the core video and Backup the extension in each `ps-uX-ltY-relearning` pack.*

Found with WebSearch limited to youtube.com, 2026-11-04. Every URL below showed up in a search result. No IDs were guessed. Notes on the method:

- **Channel**: marked confirmed only when a result's title or description named the channel (for example "...Crash Course Physics #5" or "...Physics FuseSchool"). "Channel unconfirmed" means the result didn't name the uploader. Several of those titles match Tyler DeWitt's well-known videos ("likely Tyler DeWitt"), but the search couldn't prove it. Check the channel before assigning.
- **Length**: search results almost never showed run time. The "~min" figures are from general knowledge of the series or are missing. Check that each video fits a ~4 min core plus ~6 min extension.
- **Restricted Mode**: can't be tested from here. FuseSchool, Crash Course, Khan Academy and Amoeba Sisters usually pass. Test each unconfirmed-channel video on a student account.
- **Avoid**: uploads titled like "1.1.3 (4) - 13 - Writing Formulas with Polyatomic Ions (Engl)" or "1.3.6 (1) - 2 - ... (Engl)". They copy Tyler DeWitt's bio and look like third-party re-uploads of his videos. Use the originals listed below.
